05 双极结型三极管及放大电路基础：公式总结

根据 ppt/05 双极结型三极管及放大电路基础.pdf 中出现的主要公式整理。分式、上下标、希腊字母均用数学排版渲染。

1. BJT 放大状态下的电流关系

\[ I_E=I_B+I_C \]
\[ I_C=I_{NC}+I_{CBO} \]
\[ \alpha=\frac{I_{NC}}{I_E},\qquad I_C\simeq \alpha I_E \]
\[ \beta=\frac{\alpha}{1-\alpha},\qquad \alpha=\frac{\beta}{1+\beta} \]
\[ I_C=\frac{\alpha}{1-\alpha}I_B+\frac{1}{1-\alpha}I_{CBO}=\beta I_B+(1+\beta)I_{CBO} \]
\[ I_{CEO}=(1+\beta)I_{CBO} \]
\[ I_C\simeq \beta I_B,\qquad I_B\simeq (1-\alpha)I_E,\qquad I_E\simeq I_C \]

2. 放大作用与电压增益

\[ \Delta i_C=\alpha\Delta i_E \]
\[ \Delta v_o=-\Delta i_C R_L \]
\[ A_v=\frac{\Delta v_o}{\Delta v_i} \]
\[ A_v=\frac{0.98\,\mathrm{V}}{20\,\mathrm{mV}}\approx 49 \]

3. 固定偏置共射电路静态工作点

\[ I_{BQ}=\frac{V_{BB}-V_{BEQ}}{R_b} \]
\[ I_{CQ}=\beta I_{BQ}+I_{CEO}\simeq \beta I_{BQ} \]
\[ V_{CEQ}=V_{CC}-I_{CQ}R_C \]
\[ V_{CE}=V_{CC}-i_C R_C \]
\[ v_{BE}=V_{BB}+v_s-i_BR_b \]

4. 截止、放大、饱和判据

\[ I_{CS}\simeq \frac{V_{CC}}{R_C} \]
\[ \beta I_B\lt I_{CS}\Rightarrow \text{放大区} \]
\[ \beta I_B\ge I_{CS}\Rightarrow \text{饱和区} \]
\[ I_B\simeq 0\Rightarrow \text{截止区} \]
\[ u_o=V_{CC}-I_CR_C \]

5. h 参数小信号模型

\[ v_{be}=h_{ie}i_b+h_{re}v_{ce} \]
\[ i_c=h_{fe}i_b+h_{oe}v_{ce} \]
\[ h_{ie}=\left.\frac{\partial v_{BE}}{\partial i_B}\right|_{v_{CE}},\qquad h_{re}=\left.\frac{\partial v_{BE}}{\partial v_{CE}}\right|_{i_B} \]
\[ h_{fe}=\left.\frac{\partial i_C}{\partial i_B}\right|_{v_{CE}},\qquad h_{oe}=\left.\frac{\partial i_C}{\partial v_{CE}}\right|_{i_B} \]
\[ v_{be}\simeq h_{ie}i_b,\qquad i_c\simeq \beta i_b \]

6. BJT 输入电阻 \(r_{be}\)

\[ r_{be}=r_{bb'}+(1+\beta)r_{b'e} \]
\[ r_{b'e}=\frac{V_T}{I_{EQ}} \]
\[ V_T\simeq 26\,\mathrm{mV}\quad (T=300\,\mathrm{K}) \]
\[ r_{be}\approx 200\Omega +(1+\beta)\frac{26\,\mathrm{mV}}{I_{EQ}(\mathrm{mA})} \]

7. 基本共射放大电路小信号指标

\[ v_i=i_b(R_b+r_{be}) \]
\[ i_c=\beta i_b \]
\[ v_o=-i_c(R_C\,/ /\,R_L) \]
\[ A_v=\frac{v_o}{v_i}=-\frac{\beta(R_C\,/ /\,R_L)}{R_b+r_{be}} \]
\[ \text{若 }R_b\gg r_{be},\qquad A_v\approx-\frac{\beta(R_C\,/ /\,R_L)}{r_{be}} \]
\[ R_i=R_b\,/ /\,r_{be}\approx r_{be},\qquad R_o\approx R_C \]

8. 考虑信号源内阻时的源电压增益

\[ A_{vs}=\frac{v_o}{v_s}=\frac{v_o}{v_i}\cdot\frac{v_i}{v_s}=A_v\frac{R_i}{R_s+R_i} \]

9. 分压式射极偏置电路静态工作点

\[ V_{BQ}\simeq \frac{R_{b2}}{R_{b1}+R_{b2}}V_{CC} \]
\[ I_{EQ}\simeq \frac{V_{BQ}-V_{BEQ}}{R_E} \]
\[ I_{CQ}\simeq I_{EQ},\qquad I_{BQ}=\frac{I_{CQ}}{\beta} \]
\[ V_{CEQ}=V_{CC}-I_{CQ}R_C-I_{EQ}R_E\simeq V_{CC}-I_{CQ}(R_C+R_E) \]

10. 分压式射极偏置电路小信号指标

\[ R_b=R_{b1}\,/ /\,R_{b2} \]
\[ v_i=i_b\left[r_{be}+(1+\beta)R_E\right] \]
\[ v_o=-\beta i_b(R_C\,/ /\,R_L) \]
\[ A_v=-\frac{\beta(R_C\,/ /\,R_L)}{r_{be}+(1+\beta)R_E} \]
\[ R_i=R_b\,/ /\,\left[r_{be}+(1+\beta)R_E\right],\qquad R_o\approx R_C \]
\[ \text{若 }R_E\text{ 被旁路电容交流短路，则 }A_v\approx-\frac{\beta(R_C\,/ /\,R_L)}{r_{be}} \]

11. 阻容耦合双电源射极偏置电路

\[ I_E=\frac{V_{EE}-V_{BE}}{R_E},\qquad I_C\simeq I_E,\qquad I_B=\frac{I_C}{\beta} \]
\[ V_{CE}=V_{CC}+V_{EE}-I_C(R_C+R_E) \]
\[ A_v=-\frac{\beta(R_C\,/ /\,R_L)}{r_{be}+(1+\beta)R_E} \]
\[ R_i=R_b\,/ /\,\left[r_{be}+(1+\beta)R_E\right],\qquad R_o\approx R_C \]

12. 共集电极放大电路（射极输出器）

\[ V_{CC}=I_{BQ}R_b+V_{BEQ}+I_{EQ}R_E,\qquad I_{EQ}=(1+\beta)I_{BQ} \]
\[ I_{BQ}=\frac{V_{CC}-V_{BEQ}}{R_b+(1+\beta)R_E},\qquad I_{CQ}=\beta I_{BQ} \]
\[ V_{CEQ}=V_{CC}-I_{EQ}R_E \]
\[ R_L'=R_E\,/ /\,R_L \]
\[ v_i=i_b\left[r_{be}+(1+\beta)R_L'\right],\qquad v_o=(1+\beta)i_bR_L' \]
\[ A_v=\frac{(1+\beta)R_L'}{r_{be}+(1+\beta)R_L'}=\frac{R_L'}{\dfrac{r_{be}}{1+\beta}+R_L'}\approx 1 \]
\[ R_i=R_b\,/ /\,\left[r_{be}+(1+\beta)R_L'\right] \]
\[ R_o=R_E\,/ /\,\frac{R_s+r_{be}}{1+\beta} \]

13. 共基极放大电路

\[ A_v=\frac{v_o}{v_i}=\frac{\beta(R_C\,/ /\,R_L)}{r_{be}} \]
\[ R_i=R_E\,/ /\,\frac{r_{be}}{1+\beta} \]
\[ R_o\approx R_C \]
共基电路电压增益为正，输入电阻小，输出电阻约为集电极电阻。

14. 三种基本组态的对比

组态电压增益输入电阻输出电阻
共射 \( -\dfrac{\beta(R_C\,/ /\,R_L)}{r_{be}} \) \( R_b\,/ /\,r_{be} \) \( R_C \)
共集 \( \dfrac{(1+\beta)R_L'}{r_{be}+(1+\beta)R_L'}\approx 1 \) \( R_b\,/ /\,\left[r_{be}+(1+\beta)R_L'\right] \) \( R_E\,/ /\,\dfrac{R_s+r_{be}}{1+\beta} \)
共基 \( \dfrac{\beta(R_C\,/ /\,R_L)}{r_{be}} \) \( R_E\,/ /\,\dfrac{r_{be}}{1+\beta} \) \( R_C \)

15. 组合放大电路

\[ A_v=A_{v1}A_{v2}\cdots A_{vn} \]
\[ R_i=R_{i1},\qquad R_o=R_{on} \]
\[ A_{v1}=-\frac{\beta_1R_{i2}'}{r_{be1}},\qquad A_{v2}=-\frac{\beta_2(R_C\,/ /\,R_L)}{r_{be2}+R_E'} \]
\[ A_v=A_{v1}A_{v2} \]

16. 复合管等效参数

\[ r_{be}=r_{be1}+(1+\beta_1)r_{be2} \]
\[ \beta\simeq \beta_1\beta_2 \]
\[ A_v=\frac{(1+\beta)R_L'}{r_{be}+(1+\beta)R_L'}\approx 1 \]
\[ R_i=R_{b1}\,/ /\,R_{b2}\,/ /\,\left[r_{be}+(1+\beta)R_L'\right] \]
\[ R_o=R_E\,/ /\,\frac{R_s+r_{be}}{1+\beta} \]

17. 频率响应

\[ |A_v|=|A_{vm}|\frac{1}{\sqrt{1+\left(\dfrac{f_L}{f}\right)^2}}\frac{1}{\sqrt{1+\left(\dfrac{f}{f_H}\right)^2}} \]
\[ |A_v|\approx\frac{|A_{vm}|}{\sqrt{1+\left(\dfrac{f_L}{f}\right)^2}}\quad (f\ll f_H) \]
\[ |A_v|\approx\frac{|A_{vm}|}{\sqrt{1+\left(\dfrac{f}{f_H}\right)^2}}\quad (f\gg f_L) \]
\[ f=f_L\text{ 或 }f=f_H\Rightarrow |A_v|=\frac{|A_{vm}|}{\sqrt{2}} \]
\[ 20\lg\frac{|A_v|}{|A_{vm}|}=-3\,\mathrm{dB} \]
